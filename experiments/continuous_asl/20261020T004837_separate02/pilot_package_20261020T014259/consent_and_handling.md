# Consent and data-handling checklist — all real approvals PENDING

This is an operational checklist, not a signed consent form or a claim of legal/institutional approval. The responsible human must establish applicable permissions and finalize understandable participant materials before recording. No real participant consent, signature, qualification or permission was generated by the synthetic fixtures.

Before enabling capture or reading participant artifacts, record human-reviewed evidence for:

- Project controller/contact, purpose (private ASL streaming evaluation), applicable institution/site/data-use permissions, and adult participant eligibility. Use accessible language/ASL interpretation as needed; participation and refusal must be voluntary.
- What will be captured: identifiable face/hands/body video, timestamps and derived pose/features; whether audio is needed (default off). Explain that pseudonyms/hashes do not make face/video or potentially biometric features anonymous.
- Separate explicit scopes for RECORD, LOCAL_PROCESS, ANNOTATE and EVALUATE. Training, public sharing, cloud upload, model distribution and commercial use are outside this pilot. Do not infer those permissions from an evaluation consent.
- Who can access source video, annotations, features and identity linkage; approved storage/device locations; whether any reviewer/software service would receive data. No third-party/cloud upload is authorized by this package.
- A concrete retention-until date, withdrawal/contact process, how withdrawal propagates to derivatives and any disclosed limits. Do not promise deletion from an uncontrolled copy. Keep a withdrawal log; withdrawn/expired scopes block validation/processing. This package deletes no files automatically.
- Qualification verification and a private identity/exposure audit covering prior training/evaluation/design cohorts. Do not collect unnecessary identity documents or publish a crosswalk. Resolve signer identity ambiguity explicitly; different folder labels are insufficient.
- Reviewer confidentiality/permission and annotation responsibilities, compensation if any (fill truthfully), participant questions, and confirmation that no benefits/accuracy guarantees were promised.

The coordinator maintains consent/signature documents and the signer crosswalk in access-controlled local storage outside the public repository. The manifest stores only pseudonymous references, a human reviewer ID, established/expiry times and authorized scope. A separate deidentified approval record authorizes artifact inspection after the human has checked the originals. The validator checks those attestations and dates; it **cannot authenticate consent, identity or legal authority**. Do not mark HUMAN_VERIFIED merely to unblock software.

During collection, use an approved local-only capture method, avoid bystanders/private objects, disable automatic backups, and stop if permission is withdrawn or a bystander appears. Keep raw media immutable; transformations/derived features get new IDs and documented provenance. Do not record unrelated people to create negatives.

After consented capture, hash files locally, retain original source timestamps/orientation and keep annotations/features under the same access/retention scope. Run validation locally; outputs contain only pseudonymous counts/status/error codes. Do not commit real manifests, media, consent documents, raw observations or participant features to this repository. If permission is pending, withdrawn, expired or ambiguous, stop the affected data action and record BLOCKED. Synthetic demonstrations require none of these real permissions and must remain visibly synthetic.
