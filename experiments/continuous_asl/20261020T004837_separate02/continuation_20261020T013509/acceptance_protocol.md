# Proposed streaming evaluation protocol — data gate BLOCKED

This protocol proposes research criteria; it is not a claim of measured accuracy or an instruction to change production. No new training is recommended now. Current isolated final-test rows and the exposed NCSLGR diagnostic validation remain excluded from new fitting, calibration and checkpoint selection.

1. **Freeze the comparison before scoring.** Record current baseline and candidate hashes, observation producer/model hashes, recognition-v2 width350, segmentation width378, vocabulary, raw schema, normalization, cadence, device, camera orientation, matching policy, gloss normalization, OOV policy, decoder settings and thresholds. Audit train/validation/test signer identities, raw hashes, sessions and prior exposure. Synthetic concatenations, duplicated cadence views and collection separation do not establish signer independence. Gloss spelling normalization may be documented; English translation must not generate ASL gloss targets. Preserve OOV and uncertain intervals explicitly; do not silently remove difficult references.
2. **Use natural synchronized streaming.** Require upright unmirrored RGB or authorized raw-v1 observations with genuine source timestamps and observed/missing flags, not fabricated interpolation. Derive350/378 features through the existing versioned pipeline and verify Python/Android semantics on authorized fixtures. No legacy258-to350 padding or renaming. Match source clock to analyzer, inference, commit and normal-app UI clocks; bound clock error and preserve stalls/dropped frames. Label from video before viewing predictions. Two qualified ASL reviewers independently label sign glosses, onsets/offsets and non-sign intervals; adjudicate disagreements and retain unknown/ambiguous labels. Mark non-sign with visible hands separately from no-visible-hand frames and OOV signing.
3. **Assign roles once.** Collect signers separately for future FIT, calibration VAL and untouched FINAL evaluation. Current frozen weights may be replayed on a new evaluation-only pilot without using it for tuning. The two-signer pilot below is too small to establish a complete train/val/test program. Do not later convert those evaluation sessions into training data or select an experiment using their results. A qualified final screen should include at least5 held-out signers and at least100 scorable sign events as an initial engineering floor, with a prospective precision/power calculation that may require more. These counts are proposals, not established statistical sufficiency. Report per-signer/device results and signer-cluster intervals; one pooled number cannot hide a collapsed signer.
4. **WER.** Compute `(S+D+I)/N_reference_glosses` by a pinned token-level Levenshtein implementation on final committed sequences, not classifier windows. Include empty predictions, duplicated outputs and whole eligible sessions. Also report exact-phrase accuracy, S/D/I counts, N, OOV rate and every signer. Compare both paths on identical source sessions. Proposed success:≥5 percentage-point absolute WER reduction and signer-bootstrap95% CI for candidate minus baseline entirely below0. Existing88%/92% pilots do not provide a compatible streaming acceptance baseline. With insufficient signer clusters, report exploratory metrics and leave the gate BLOCKED.
5. **False activations.** Count every final commit entirely within an adjudicated negative interval; count duplicates separately and as emitted errors. Divide by eligible negative wall-clock minutes, not windows, frames or available model calls. Define a fixed boundary guard band before scoring and disclose excluded time; uncertain intervals do not become negatives. Report idle hands, face-adjacent activity, accidental motion, no-visible-hands and OOV separately; OOV is an unsupported-sign rejection measure, not absence of signing. Proposed target: one-sided95% upper exposure bound≤0.1 false commits/min. With zero events, the homogeneous Poisson planning bound is `-ln(.05)/T`;30minutes gives0.09986/min. This is a model-based bound, not proof of temporal or participant independence. Include per-signer rates and cluster-based paired comparisons; collect longer exposure if events occur. A brief clean clip cannot pass this criterion.
6. **Missed signs.** Before lexical comparison, match inferred committed events to reference sign events one-to-one in chronological order by interval IoU≥0.5; report matching sensitivity to the predeclared tolerance. An unmatched scorable sign is a miss. A matched wrong gloss is a WER substitution, not silently a correct detection. Report no-event misses, incorrect-label events, correct-but-dropped emissions and duplicate events separately. Proposed success: miss fraction and its one-sided95% upper confidence bound≤10%; use a declared signer-aware uncertainty method, not independent-frame sample sizes. Insufficient data leaves qualification BLOCKED.
7. **Boundaries.** Obtain actual inferred onset/offset intervals as well as commit time. Evaluate one-to-one event precision/recall/F1 at IoU≥0.5, temporal onset/offset absolute-error p50/p95, early/late signed errors and duplicates. Proposed targets: eventF1≥0.90, onset and offset p95≤200ms each, duplicate committed events≤1% of references. Include hands-up rearming, repeated signs, sign-then-hold, transitions/partials and ordinary movements. Commit time alone does not identify an offset. If the current frozen path does not export a required timestamp, mark that metric NOT_MEASURED; do not infer it from a posterior maximum.
8. **End-to-end latency.** On matched correct commits report source-sign-onset→delivery and source-sign-end→delivery p50/p95. Preserve negative end-relative times for early commits; do not clamp them away. Count misses separately so a model that emits nothing cannot look fast. Also report camera→observation, perception, materialization, inference, confirmation and UI-delivery delays, warm-up, queueing, gaps, memory and completed observation FPS. Proposed target: sign-end→normal-app final delivery p95≤1000ms, with completed processing≥15FPS over a5-minute session on the declared target phone. Report slow or degraded intervals explicitly. Laptop model-only ONNX timings and diagnostic-Activity UI timings cannot pass a normal-app end-to-end gate. No phone build, asset change or APK installation is authorized here.

No production qualification is granted by these proposed criteria. Freeze their final values and the target domain before a new independent evaluation; thresholds must not be fitted on FINAL sessions. Rights, consent, schema parity, supervision, exposure lineage and signer separation are prerequisites even when the mathematical training loss can be computed.
