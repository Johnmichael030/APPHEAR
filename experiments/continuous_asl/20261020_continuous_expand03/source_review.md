[BU continuous-corpus terms](https://www.bu.edu/asllrp/dai-terms.html) allow research/education, forbid redistribution without permission, prohibit commercial use without explicit permission and restrict patents/copyright based on the material. Keep all acquired media/derived data/checkpoints private/local. Cite Neidle/Vogler2012 and Neidle/Opoku/Metaxas2022 and corpus URLs in publications; this report makes no distribution/deployment-rights claim. These existing corpus terms govern the public research data; no new participant was recorded and no consent for the separate phone pilot was fabricated.

[Official annotation conventions](https://www.bu.edu/asllrp/asllrpr11.pdf) document gloss notation including index/possessive/locus forms, holds and particles. The new tokenizer preserves complete index strings verbatim, including case and punctuation within admitted tokens. It excludes quotes/descriptive classifiers/explicit fingerspelling instead of splitting embedded descriptions into invented lexical labels. HOLD/grammar markers remain as original annotation targets, so the metric is literal source-main-gloss-token WER rather than a claim of standardized lexical-only gloss accuracy.

The publisher warns some compressed movies play at half speed. Full source/annotation joins support sequence diagnostics, but container cadence is not independent real-world sign timing truth. No boundary/no-sign labels are derived from the index, model blank or absent hand observations. Next richer source is an authorized DAI XML/participant export; this would enable signer-aware splits and timing checks rather than another ungrounded cadence sweep.
