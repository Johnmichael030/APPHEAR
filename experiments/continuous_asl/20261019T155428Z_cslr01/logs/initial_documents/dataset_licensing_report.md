# Dataset eligibility, 2026-10-20 Asia/Shanghai

Status: DOCUMENTATION_REVIEWED; availability of acquired files must be checked separately.

- **How2Sign:** official page identifies ASL continuous video with translation and keypoint modalities, under CC BY-NC4.0/research-only use. Attribution/change notices required; commercial use is not authorized. The current official downloader lists gloss annotations as TODO. Eligible here for a small metadata inspection, not gloss-CTC training without authentic glosses. No right to redistribute video in this repository is asserted.
- **OpenASL:** official repository identifies ASL-English translation and CC BY-NC-ND4.0. Excluded from active acquisition/training pending clarification of permitted training/derived-model use; do not infer derivative rights.
- **ASL Citizen:** isolated ASL, not continuous sequence supervision. Microsoft directs commercial enquiries to its project team. Exact dataset agreement must be reviewed before a new auxiliary run; no42.8GB corpus download is scheduled.
- **WLASL:** isolated words; C-UDA agreement and academic/non-commercial restrictions. Excluded from active training until agreement review; source videos can be unavailable.
- **BU NCSLGR / ASLLRP:** authentic continuous ASL with timed gloss/nonmanual annotations. Official terms permit research/education, restrict redistribution and commercial use, and include a patents/copyright restriction. Eligible for private non-commercial data feasibility only. No dataset, derivative-model distribution or production deployment rights are asserted. Keep source/derived data/checkpoints local; obtain clarification before distribution.
- Other sign languages are excluded as interchangeable ASL labels. The How2Sign catalogue is a discovery source, not a license grant.
